Our Origin

The Architecture of Quiet Luxury

Founded in 2022, LUXEWEAR was conceived out of a fundamental desire: to liberate luxury fashion from transient novelty and return it to the enduring sanctity of extraordinary materials and impeccable cuts.

With dual creative ateliers in Milan and Lagos, our collections celebrate the sophisticated cadence of modern African and international tastemakers. We reject excessive logos in favor of silhouette purity, drape, and the unmistakable touch of 22-momme Mulberry silk, Italian virgin wool, and European flax linen.

I.

Material Integrity

We curate exclusively from historic mills across Como, Biella, and Normandy. Natural virgin wools, organic linens, and vegetable-tanned leathers that soften and enrich with age.

II.

Artisanal Tailoring

From hand-rolled French seams to floating chest canvases and horn button fastenings, each garment is executed by master patternmakers with surgical precision.

III.

Conscious Restraint

We produce in disciplined, limited-run batches. By circumventing runaway overproduction, our garments retain their exclusivity and honor environmental stewardship.

"True luxury does not clamor for attention. It speaks in the subtle perfection of an unlined lapel, the crisp breath of linen in the tropical breeze, and the confidence of knowing your clothing is second to none."
Tariq & Adanna Cole Founders & Creative Directors, LUXEWEAR
